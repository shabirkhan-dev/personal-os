The engineering roadmap for Personal OS. The board in `.agents/board/` tracks individual cards;
this page is the summary. Architecture context: [Product System Design](/docs/product-system-design).

## Built

### Foundation

- Bun + Turborepo monorepo, oxlint/oxfmt, Lefthook hooks, architecture and naming checks, CI,
  CodeQL and Dependabot.
- `@personal-os/tokens`: Figma Kit v2 colours, spacing, radii and text styles, light and dark.

### Mobile app

- Onboarding end to end: voice input, saved progress, reminders, plan reveal and sign-in.
- Today: Overview plus a Finance Home, and Profile, all on what the person enters. Module data is stored on the phone
  ([ADR 0001](/docs/architecture/adr-0001-on-device-module-data)).
- Ask bar that parses typed lines into module entries.
- Career module: pipeline, follow-ups with AI-drafted nudges, job links read by AI with a halal
  reading, interview prep with flashcards, and a 30-minute-a-day skill path.
- Faith module: prayer times on the phone (location or typed times) with reminders, a Sky header
  that follows the time of day, prayer logging and consistency, Qibla compass, Tasbih, Quran
  recitation timer, ayah and dua cards read aloud, Zakat, Grow (challenges, paths, flashcards,
  notes) and Insights, plus questions answered by the Faith AI.
- Fashion module: wardrobe with photos (starter photos, camera, gallery or a product link read by
  AI), today's look picked on the phone for the weather (Open-Meteo) with layer advice, a watch and
  a scent, a rotating week plan, piece detail with cost per wear, wishlist with a buying pause,
  watches and scents, and Insights.
- Food module: meals logged from a line, Say it, a photo or a barcode (Open Food Facts), matched
  on the phone with the Food AI reading what it doesn't know; goal and targets from height,
  weight and age; macros, weight and water on Home; a week's meal plan with costs, a grocery list
  in whole packs against a weekly budget, recipes with photos and cook mode read aloud, and
  Insights. Synced through the record store.
- Care module (skin, hair, body): a routine built from what the person wants to work on, morning
  and night routines ticked on Home or one step at a time read aloud, today's UV with a sunscreen
  reapply time, the shelf with how long each product lasts and what to reorder, hair and scalp,
  body and nails with daily habits (water shared with Food), a weekly check-in with no photos,
  and Insights. Synced through the record store.
- Training (was Workout): a week program built from the sports picked and days a week, with
  missed sessions moved to an easy day; session plans with photos, a live session with sets,
  the number to beat and a rest timer, boxing rounds with call-outs read aloud, run summaries
  with pace and splits, skills as step ladders (the Training AI builds new ones), personal bests,
  phases and Insights. Synced through the record store.
- Daily life, the hub that ties the modules together: the minimum day (3 to 5 small things the
  OS protects, with a "never miss twice" streak), what's on right now, every module's line for
  today and the rest of the day; a morning brief, a day plan drafted from the modules, closing
  the day with energy and one good thing, sleep with a wind-down and alarms, and Insights.
  Modules feed it through one small hook each.
- - Voice in and out on the phone behind one swappable layer (`src/lib/voice`), and Talk to OS
  (`personalos://listen`), which opens listening and hands the line to the right module.
- "Hey OS" while the app is open (on-device speech, off until turned on in Profile), and a Talk to
  OS launcher shortcut on Android.
- Profile photo (camera or gallery, kept on the phone and uploaded to R2 when signed in) and name
  synced to the account; fields and sheets stay above the keyboard everywhere.
- Long AI work runs in the background with a working card, so no screen waits on it.
- Offline-first record store on the phone with sync to the API
  ([ADR 0002](/docs/architecture/adr-0002-offline-first-sync)).
- EAS development builds, EAS Update channels and a one-command APK build.

### API and services

- One Hono API on Cloudflare Workers ([ADR 0003](/docs/architecture/adr-0003-one-api-on-workers)):
  Neon through Hyperdrive, Cloudflare rate limiting, typed config and one table of error codes.
- Email-code sign-in with rotating refresh sessions.
- Profile with avatars uploaded straight to R2; onboarding answers stored and synced.
- Career AI inside the API, with a mock provider for development and tests.
- Landing page on Cloudflare Workers with a launch waitlist.

## Next

Roughly in order; each item becomes board cards.

1. **Remaining modules**: Wishlist, on the record store.
1. **Piece and meal photos to R2** so a wardrobe and food log sync with their photos (they stay on the phone for now).
1. **"Hey OS" with the app closed**: today it listens while the app is open (Profile → Hey OS) and
   Android has a Talk to OS launcher shortcut. Waking from the background needs an on-device
   wake-word engine in an Android foreground service; iOS does not allow always-on listening.
2. **Move Finance onto the record store** so everything is synced.
3. **Files**: an avatar picker and photos through the R2 upload flow; videos streamed from Google
   Drive.
4. **Insights**: AI over a per-module, opt-in daily digest, shown in the Insights tab.
5. **Production hardening**: error tracking, migrations in the deploy pipeline, and real deploy
   steps in `cd.yml` (it is still a template).
6. **Desktop app** on the same record store and sync.

## Engineering rules

- Every API change updates [Backend API](/docs/backend-api) in the same commit.
- Schema changes ship with a generated migration; no schema push outside local development.
- Pure logic gets unit tests; screens handle loading, empty and error states.
- No secrets in the app bundle; provider keys are Worker secrets.
- `bun run preflight` passes before a push.
