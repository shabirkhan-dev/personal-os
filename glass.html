How the bend actually works

The glass never samples what's underneath. Instead a small generated image — the displacement map — tells an SVG feDisplacementMap filter which way to push each pixel of the real content that's already there. Red and green channels store an X and Y offset; a neutral grey means "leave this pixel alone," which is why everything outside the lens stays untouched. Shape changes (width, radius, depth, curvature, splay) regenerate that map; look changes (scale, chroma, blur, glow…) just tweak filter attributes in place, which is why those stay cheap enough to animate every frame.
